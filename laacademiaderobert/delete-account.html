Delete your account

«La academia de Robert» app (DAM platform), published by Roberto del Cid, doing business as Del Cid Solutions («the platform»)

If the two versions differ, the Spanish one prevails.

You can ask us to delete your app account and your personal information at any time. It is free.

1. Who handles your request

The academy, La academia de Robert, is responsible for your student information. The platform, which runs the academy's system, handles the email in the Contact section together with the academy, carries the deletion out and is the contact for your app account and technical support. We inform the academy's management of every request, and it remains the one answerable to you for your student information.

2. How to request it

  1. Write to us from the email you signed up with, to the address in the Contact section, with the subject «Delete my account». If you no longer have access to that email, you can ask in person at the academy's front desk, and they will pass it on to us.
  2. Include your full name and the phone number you signed up with.
  3. We confirm that we received your request and check that you own the account before deleting anything. If you are a minor, you or your parent or guardian can make the request.

If you have an active membership or future bookings, we first cancel them with the academy: deleting the account does not refund credits or payments, and your membership rules are still the academy's.

3. What is deleted

  • Your access to the app: the email and password you sign in with, and all your open sessions.
  • Your profile information: name, phone, contact email, date of birth, gender, dance role, language, appearance and the academy's notes about you.
  • The SMS verification records that hold your number.
  • Your consents for news and for photos and videos are withdrawn.

4. What is kept

  • Bookings, attendance, credits and payments. They are the academy's accounting records and cannot be erased without breaking its books. They stay linked to a file that carries no name or contact details once the deletion is complete, for as long as the law requires the academy to keep them.
  • That your phone already used the free class. It is kept as a code that does not show the number, so the free class stays one per person. It is kept for as long as the law requires the academy to keep it.
  • The record of what you accepted and of your deletion request, to prove it was done, for as long as the law requires the academy to keep it.
  • Backups last up to 30 days: your information disappears from them when they expire.
  • Server technical logs hold no personal data and are deleted automatically after 30 days.

5. How long it takes

We confirm receipt the same day, with the date we received it, and complete it within five business days, the period set by Costa Rica's Law No. 8968 (article 7). If we need more information to find your account, we ask for it once, and the period starts again when you reply. We let you know by email when it is done, or explain why if something cannot be deleted.

6. If you only want to stop receiving news

You do not need to delete your account: ask the academy or write to us to withdraw your consent for news or for photos and videos.

7. Contact

In writing, there is a single contact, for your student information, your app account and technical support: roberto.delcid@gmail.com. The platform handles it, together with the academy.

For your student information you can also ask in person at the academy's administration.

More details in the Privacy policy. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can go to the Agency for the Protection of Inhabitants' Data (PRODHAB).
