Privacy policy

«La academia de Robert» app (DAM platform) · Version 1 · Effective November 13, 2026

If the two versions differ, the Spanish one prevails.

This policy explains, in plain language, what information about you the app uses, why, who sees it, where it is stored, for how long, and how to exercise your rights. It complements the privacy notice you accept in the app.

1. Who is who and how to contact us

Two parties are involved in this app, and each one answers for different things:

  • The academy, La academia de Robert, is responsible for your information as a student: it decides what your file, classes, bookings, attendance, membership and payments are used for. Your relationship as a student is with the academy.
  • Roberto del Cid, doing business as Del Cid Solutions (on this page, «the platform»), develops and runs DAM, the system the academy uses, and publishes this app in the stores. The platform does two things:
    • It stores and processes your student information on behalf of the academy, only for what this policy says, and never for purposes of its own.
    • It is directly responsible for your sign-in account (email, password, sessions and the verification of your phone) and for running the platform (security, technical logs and app updates).
  • DAM serves several academies. Each academy's information is kept apart: one academy's staff cannot see another academy's information.

In writing, there is a single contact: roberto.delcid@gmail.com. The platform handles it, together with the academy.

  • For your student information (to see it, correct it, withdraw consents or ask for its deletion): write to that address, and the platform resolves it together with the academy. You can also ask in person at the academy's administration.
  • For your app account and technical support (signing in, password, phone verification, app problems): write to the same address.

In plain words, two technical items are collected automatically: a device identifier (a random code the app creates on your phone the first time and keeps there, sent each time the app checks for an update) and a crash log (if the app closes because of an error, the technical text of that last error, sent with the next update check). Both go only to Expo, our update provider, which handles them on our behalf to deliver updates and to tell us whether one failed. We do not share them with anyone else, we do not use them for advertising or to follow you, and they are not linked to your name or account.

3. What we do NOT use

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. It has no ads, no advertising identifier, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not store card data. We do not sell your information.

If you add a class to your calendar, the event is created on your phone; the app does not read your calendar or send it anywhere.

4. Who sees your information

  • You see your own information.
  • The academy's management (owner and administrators) sees your file: name, phone, email, bookings, attendance, membership and payments.
  • Your teachers see, only on the day of the class, the name and dance role of the people booked in that class.
  • The platform has technical access to run and support the system, and uses it only for that.

5. Providers that help us

We use these providers to run the service. They receive only what is needed and act on our behalf:

  • Microsoft Azure: hosting of the server, the database, backups and technical logs (United States, East US 2 region).
  • Twilio: sending the SMS with the verification code; it receives your number and the message text (United States).
  • Expo: delivering app updates; it receives the update technical data in section 2 and the phone's IP address (United States).
  • Google Maps: shows the map of the academy's location; it receives your phone's IP address when the map loads, not your location.

When the app adds card payments or notifications, this policy will be updated before they are turned on.

6. Information outside Costa Rica

The servers and the SMS and update providers are in the United States. By accepting the privacy notice, you authorize your information to be stored and processed there with the safeguards in this policy.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and student information is kept until you ask us to delete it, or for as long as the law requires it to be kept. There is no automatic deletion today: if you stop going to the academy, your information stays stored until you ask for its deletion (see Delete your account).
  • If you ask for deletion, your bookings, credits, and payments, and the record of your free class, are kept as the academy's records; once the deletion is complete, they no longer carry your name. They are kept for as long as the law requires the academy to keep them.
  • Server technical logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Backups last up to 30 days: deleted information disappears from them when they expire.

8. Minors

If your academy does not accept minors, you must be 18 to sign up.

If you are a minor, to book classes you need the authorization of your parent or guardian, given in person at the academy; until the academy can record it, you will not be able to book. When you sign up, only your name, date of birth, verified phone, email and password are stored. The rest of your information and consents wait for that validation.

9. Your rights

Under Costa Rica's Law No. 8968 on the Protection of Individuals with regard to the Processing of their Personal Data, you can find out what information we hold about you, correct what is wrong, withdraw your consents for news and for photos and videos, and request deletion of your account and information.

How it works today: inside the app you can only change the language and the appearance. Everything else is requested by writing to us (section 1) or at the academy's front desk. To delete your account, follow Delete your account.

Before acting we verify your identity, so nobody can request changes to someone else's account. We confirm that we received your request and answer within five business days, the period set by law (Law No. 8968, article 7). If we need more information to find your account, we ask for it once, and the period starts again when you reply. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can go to the Agency for the Protection of Inhabitants' Data (PRODHAB).

10. How we protect your information

All communication between the app and the server is encrypted. The password is stored with a one-way method. Your session is kept in the phone's secure storage. Server technical logs do not store personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we publish the new version on this same page with its date. If the change affects what you accepted, we will ask you again in the app.
